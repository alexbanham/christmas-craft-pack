# Christmas Craft

A Minecraft modpack for a group of friends, played from November to Christmas.
Create and airships, a casino with real chips, Santa's Toy Works, the Yuletide Forge, eight spirit bosses, Advent and a quest book with 439 quests.
Download it once. It updates itself every time you press Play.

## Install

1. **Unzip it.** Open your Downloads folder and find `ChristmasCraft-Launcher.zip`. Right-click it and choose **Extract All...**. Click **Browse**, pick **Documents**, then click **Extract**. A new folder called `ChristmasCraft-Launcher` opens.
   - Don't just double-click the zip: that only shows what's inside, and the launcher won't run from there.
   - Not Program Files, and not a OneDrive folder.
2. **Start the launcher.** In that new folder, double-click `prismlauncher.exe` (it may show as just `prismlauncher`, with a blue cube icon). If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Next time, start it from the same place, or right-click it and choose **Pin to Start**.
3. Sign in with your Microsoft account when it asks.
4. Double-click **Christmas Craft**.

The first start downloads Java, Minecraft, NeoForge and about 250 mods: about 2 GB. Give it 5 to 15 minutes. A small window shows the progress.

## Updates

Every time you press Play, the launcher checks for updates and installs them before the game starts. You never download the zip again.

To play together, everyone must be on the same version. If in doubt, close the game and press Play again.

## Joining the server

1. Start Christmas Craft and click **Multiplayer**.
2. Click **Add Server** and enter the address Alex posted in Discord.
3. Click **Done**, then join.

Voice chat: Simple Voice Chat is in the pack. Press **V** in game to set it up.

## If something goes wrong

- **Windows says it protected your PC.** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Some browsers also warn about the zip: choose to keep it.
- **Odd errors, or nothing saves.** The folder is probably inside OneDrive or Program Files, or the launcher was opened from inside the zip. Extract the zip to Documents and open `prismlauncher.exe` from there.
- **The first start is slow.** That is the 2 GB download. Give it 5 to 15 minutes.
- **A window says a download failed.** Close it and press Play again. It carries on where it stopped.
- **"Incompatible client" when joining.** You are on a different version. Close the game and press Play in the launcher so it updates. The friend you are joining does the same.
- **Low frame rate.** Shaders are on by default. Turn them off in Options > Video Settings > Shader Packs.
- **PC with only 8 GB of memory.** Turn the shaders off. In the launcher, right-click Christmas Craft > Edit > Settings > Memory and set the maximum to 5000.
