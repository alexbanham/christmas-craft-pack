How to play

  1. 1

    Download the zip above and unzip it anywhere, like your Desktop.

  2. 2

    Open prismlauncher.exe inside the folder.

  3. 3

    Click Accounts (top right), then Manage accounts, then Add Microsoft, and sign in.

  4. 4

    Double-click Christmas Craft. The first launch downloads Minecraft and the mods. After that, updates install by themselves.

You need: Windows and the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition (bought, or included with PC Game Pass). Never download the zip again: just press Play.
